## 📌 Executive Summary
Validating new digital commerce concepts often requires weeks of primary market research, persona creation, and financial modeling. This project demonstrates an **AI-accelerated 60-minute venture stress-test pipeline**, evaluating a dual-offering pet care ecosystem: **AT Food Co.** (Fresh Meal Subscriptions + Digital "Dog Life Record").

By leveraging structural prompt architectures (**CO-STAR**), synthetic customer personas, and adversarial AI role-playing (Skeptical VC simulation), this framework stress-tests market demand, uncovers unit-economic vulnerabilities, and auto-generates investor-ready presentation assets.

---

## 🏗️ The Business Concept: AT Food Co.
- **Core Problem:** Pet parents face conflicting nutrition information, severe digestive uncertainty with commercial kibble, and fragmented health tracking.
- **The Dual-Product Solution:** 
  1. **Physical Product:** Fresh, portion-controlled, chef-prepared meal subscriptions delivered daily/weekly.
  2. **Digital Moat:** A "Dog Life Record" tracking nutrition, digestion, allergies, and health milestones.
- **The Core Flywheel:** Meal consumption data updates the *Dog Life Record* $\rightarrow$ AI algorithms optimize next week's meal portioning $\rightarrow$ Improved pet health increases customer retention and lifetime value (LTV).
